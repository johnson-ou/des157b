The air we share

the weight we cannot see

Every breath you take contains traces of every fire ever lit.

Every drive. Every meal. Every search. Every package. Every air conditioned room.

All of it sends carbon into the only sky we have.

It used to disperse faster than we made it.

where it comes from

Five chimneys,
one sky.

global CO₂ emissions, by sector →

01 34%

Energy

Coal, oil and gas burned to make electricity and heat. The single largest source, and the one we know how to replace.

02 24%

Industry

Cement, steel, chemicals. The skeleton of the modern world, and also the hardest carbon to scrub.

03 22%

Agriculture & Land

Cows, rice paddies, fertilizer, cleared forests. The carbon hiding inside the food we eat.

04 15%

Transportation

Cars, trucks, planes, ships. The carbon of getting us from place to place.

05 5%

Buildings

Direct emissions from heating, cooking, and cooling. Quieter, but everywhere.

what we stand to lose

warmer oceans

Coral reefs that took thousands of years to grow are bleaching in a single summer.

thinner ice

The Arctic is losing roughly 12% of its summer sea ice every decade.

wilder weather

Heat waves, droughts, and floods that used to be once-a-century are arriving once a decade.

quieter forests

About 1 million species face extinction this century, and many before we even name them.
